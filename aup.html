1. Purpose and scope

This Acceptable Use Policy (the “Policy”) sets out the conduct, data, and security requirements that apply to the Avancir cloud services platform (the “Services”), including the Avancir web application, mobile and handheld applications, any downloadable software components provided as part of the Services, and the Avancir application programming interfaces and webhooks (the “APIs”).

This Policy is incorporated by reference into the Avancir Software Cloud Services Agreement (the “Agreement”) between you and Atlas RFID Solutions Store, LLC (“Atlas,” “we,” or “us”). Capitalized terms not defined in this Policy have the meanings given in the Agreement. If any provision of this Policy conflicts with the Agreement, the Agreement controls.

The purpose of this Policy is straightforward: the Services are used to track physical assets, inventory, and operations, often in regulated environments. This Policy defines what may and may not be done with the Services, and what may and may not be put into them, so that your use does not create legal exposure for you, for us, or for other customers.

This Policy does not apply to Avancir Print, which is governed by its own separate terms of use.

You must make Authorized Users aware of this Policy and ensure that they comply with it. A violation of this Policy by any such person is a violation by you.

4. Restricted data

The Services are designed to hold operational data about physical items, assets, inventory, containers, locations, and the personnel who handle them. Certain categories of data must not be submitted to the Services, in any field, record, import, attachment, note, or API payload.

4.1 Restricted Data

You may not submit to the Services:

  • Protected health information. Any protected health information (“PHI”) as defined under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 and its implementing regulations, as amended (“HIPAA”), or any equivalent category of health or medical information under other applicable law. This prohibition applies unless and until Atlas and you have executed a written business associate agreement expressly covering the Services. Atlas is not acting as a business associate and the Services are not configured, offered, or represented as HIPAA-compliant. If you track medical devices, pharmaceuticals, laboratory assets, or other healthcare inventory, you must do so without associating those items with any identifiable patient or with any individual’s health condition, treatment, or care.
  • Payment card and financial account data. Cardholder data or sensitive authentication data as those terms are used in the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard, and bank or other financial account numbers or credentials. The Services are not within the scope of any PCI DSS assessment.
  • Nonpublic personal information. Nonpublic personal information as defined in the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (15 U.S.C. § 6809(4)), including any financial information obtained in connection with providing a financial product or service to an individual.
  • Biometric information. Biometric identifiers or biometric information, including fingerprints, voiceprints, retina or iris scans, facial geometry, hand or palm geometry, and gait or other behavioral biometric data.
  • Government identifiers. Social Security numbers, taxpayer identification numbers, driver’s license or state identification numbers, passport numbers, and other government-issued identification numbers.
  • Data about children. Personal information of individuals known to be under sixteen (16) years of age, including any personal information of a child under thirteen (13) subject to the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act or any comparable law.
  • Education records. Education records and personally identifiable information from education records as those terms are used in the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (20 U.S.C. § 1232g). Tracking an asset assigned to or handled by a student under Section 5 does not create an education record, provided no academic, disciplinary, or other record covered by that Act is submitted to the Services.
  • Export-controlled technical data. Technical data, technology, software, or source code controlled under the International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR) or classified under an Export Control Classification Number on the Commerce Control List of the Export Administration Regulations (EAR), other than items designated EAR99, in each case unless Atlas has agreed in writing to receive it. Item descriptions, part numbers, serial numbers, and quantities that are not themselves controlled technical data are permitted.
  • Other special categories. Special categories of personal data under the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation (including data revealing racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, trade union membership, health, sex life, or sexual orientation), and data relating to criminal convictions or offenses.
  • Classified information. Information classified under any national security classification scheme, and controlled unclassified information, unless Atlas has agreed in writing to receive it.

You are responsible for implementing controls, including field configuration, import validation, and user training, sufficient to keep Restricted Data out of the Services. If Restricted Data is submitted to the Services, you must notify Atlas promptly at the address in Section 11 and cooperate with Atlas to remove it.

4.2 Regulatory systems of record

The Services are not offered as, and must not be relied upon as, a system of record for compliance with any regulatory regime, including drug supply chain security, food traceability, medical device unique device identification, aerospace or defense traceability, or customs and trade compliance requirements. You are solely responsible for determining whether your use of the Services satisfies any such requirement and for maintaining any separate records the law requires.

5. Tracking of individuals

RFID, IoT, and related technologies can be used to determine the location and movement of people as well as of items. This section governs any use of the Services that identifies, locates, or tracks an identifiable individual, whether directly or by inference from tagged items, badges, equipment, or containers associated with that individual.

Permitted scope. You may use the Services to identify, locate, or track an individual only where that individual is one of your employees, contractors, agents, patients, residents, students, or visitors, or a person handling your assets under a contractual relationship, and only in connection with your legitimate internal business operations, such as asset accountability, workflow and work-in-progress tracking, site access, safety, or loss prevention.

Lawful basis and notice. You must have a valid legal basis for any such tracking, and you must provide all notices and obtain all consents, authorizations, and approvals required by applicable law before the tracking begins. Depending on where you operate, this may include employee notice and consent requirements, collective bargaining or works council consultation, visitor or patient notice, and specific state or national statutes governing the tracking of workers or the use of RFID on persons or identification documents.

Covert tracking prohibited. You may not use the Services to track any individual covertly, or in a manner that a reasonable person in that individual’s position would not expect. You may not use the Services to track any individual who is not within the permitted scope above, including members of the general public, and you may not use the Services to track any individual in a private space such as a restroom, changing area, medical examination room, or residence.

Prohibited purposes. You may not use the Services to track or profile an individual for the purpose of discriminating against that individual on any basis protected by law, interfering with protected concerted or union activity, retaliating against any individual for a protected activity, monitoring an individual outside the scope of their duties and working time, or implanting or requiring the implantation of any tag or device in a person’s body.

Your responsibility. As between you and Atlas, you are the controller or owner of all personal information you submit to or generate within the Services. You are solely responsible for determining the lawfulness of your tracking program, for responding to individual rights requests, and for any claim arising from your tracking of individuals. Atlas does not review or approve your tracking program and provides no assurance that any particular use is lawful.

The Services support the import of data through CSV and spreadsheet files, through the APIs, and through manual entry and device capture. With respect to all data you submit:

  • You must have all rights, licenses, and permissions necessary to submit the data to the Services and to allow Atlas to process it in order to provide the Services.

  • You are responsible for the accuracy, completeness, and lawfulness of the data, and for validating imported files before import.
